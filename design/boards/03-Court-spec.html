Zones & placement

Each team faces the net. Front row by the net 4 · 3 · 2 (left → right from the player's view), back row 5 · 6 · 1. The far team therefore reads reversed on screen — same layout, rotated 180°.

Mini cards (72 × 92) centre on a 3 × 2 grid per half: columns at 1/6, 3/6, 5/6 of width; rows at ¼ and ¾ of each half. The attack-line band is a darker orange so front/back rows read at a glance.

Drag-to-swap

Long-press 250ms or drag past 8px: card lifts (1.05, −3°, drop-shadow, haptic). Origin = white dashed slot. Hovered slot = pink dashed + brand-soft fill + “تبديل ⇅” on the occupant.

Drop settles in 220ms; occupant flies to the origin in 260ms (FLIP); team ratings and win % count to new values. Across the net = change teams. Tap-tap also works. Chemistry lines fade to 30% while dragging.
